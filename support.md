# AdBlock: Simple Support

AdBlock: Simple blocks ads and trackers in Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
On your device and in Safari it is called AdBlock. It needs iOS or iPadOS
26.2 or later, or macOS 26 or later, and works only in Safari.

Questions or problems: [invariancestudios@gmail.com](mailto:invariancestudios@gmail.com). Please say which
device and system version you use, and what you expected to happen.

## Turn AdBlock on in Safari

Safari keeps extensions switched off until you turn them on. Open AdBlock
once first, so it can prepare its rules.

**iPhone and iPad.** Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.

1. Open *AdBlock — Ads* and turn on *Allow Extension*. Do the same for
   *AdBlock — Trackers*, and for *AdBlock — Annoyances* if you want fewer
   cookie notices and pop-ups.
2. For YouTube ads and the advanced rules, open *AdBlock*, turn on *Allow
   Extension* and set *Other Websites* to *Allow*. Set *youtube.com*,
   *youtube-nocookie.com* and *youtubekids.com* to *Allow* as well.

**Mac.** Open Safari › Settings › Extensions.

1. Turn on *AdBlock Ads*, *AdBlock Trackers* and, if you want it, *AdBlock
   Annoyances*.
2. Turn on *AdBlock*, then choose *Edit Websites…* and allow it on all
   websites.
3. In AdBlock's menu-bar menu, turn on *Open at Login* to keep the filter
   lists up to date without opening the app.

Safari profiles and Private Browsing have their own extension settings.

Back in the app, the main screen says *Protection is on* once Safari has
loaded AdBlock's current rules. If something is missing, it names the
switch to turn on.

## Allow a website

If a website does not work properly with blocking, allow it:

- in Safari: on iPhone and iPad, open the page menu in the address bar and
  choose AdBlock; on the Mac, click AdBlock's toolbar button. Then choose
  *Allow ads on this site* and reload the page. *Block ads on this site*
  undoes it.
- in the app: open *Websites*, enter the address and choose *Add Website*.
  With *Include subdomains* on, `example.com` also covers
  `www.example.com` and `shop.example.com`. *Remove* takes a website off
  the list.

To stop blocking everywhere for a while, switch *Protection* off on the
main screen. Your settings are kept, and switching it on again resumes
blocking.

## YouTube

AdBlock removes ads from YouTube videos played in Safari, not in the
YouTube app. It needs *AdBlock — Ads* (or *AdBlock Ads* on the Mac) and the
*AdBlock* extension, allowed on YouTube's websites as described above.

## Filter updates

AdBlock includes its filter lists, so blocking works offline from the first
launch. It checks AdGuard's filter service for newer lists about once a day
when you open it. On iPhone and iPad it also checks when the system gives it
background time, and on the Mac while it runs in the menu bar. *Update* on
the main screen checks right away.

If an update fails, AdBlock keeps using the rules Safari already loaded.
Check your internet connection and try *Update* again later. If it keeps
failing, open Advanced › Diagnostics, share the report and send it to the
address above. The report contains no browsing history; your website list
is included only if you choose to add it.

## Privacy

AdBlock has no account, analytics or tracking, and nothing about the
websites you visit leaves your device. See the
